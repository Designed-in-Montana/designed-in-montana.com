About

A directory for the businesses that make Montana.

Small operations, big country. This is where they get listed, linked, and shared.

Designed in Montana exists to help small Montana businesses — both the ones with a storefront on Main Street and the ones operating entirely online — get noticed for what they do and what they represent about this state.

Montana is more than scenery. It's the people building things under the Big Sky: the woodworker outside Bozeman, the candle-maker in Lewistown, the IT shop in Fairfield, the photographer in Miles City, the rancher selling beef direct from Glasgow. They're often small. They're often invisible to anyone who doesn't already know them. That's the problem this directory was built to chip away at.

What this site is

A simple, hand-curated directory and backlink hub. Every business listed here gets a card with their logo, a short description, links to their website and socials, an address that opens in a map, a clickable phone number, and a clickable email. That's it. No paid placements, no rankings, no clutter — just a clean catalog organized by Montana's five tourism regions, then by county and town.

Who it's for

For visitors looking past the chain stores. For Montanans who'd rather buy from a neighbor. For business owners who want a real backlink and a fair listing. For anyone who thinks "designed in Montana" should mean something specific — and worth pointing to.

How to get listed

There are a few requirements — your business needs to be registered with the Montana Secretary of State, have a real website, and have an email tied to that website. The full list is on the requirements page. After that, just reach out.
