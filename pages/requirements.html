Listing Requirements

What it takes to get listed.

A short list to make sure every business here is a real, reachable Montana operation.

Work in progress. This list will grow as the directory matures. For now, these are the baseline requirements — additional standards may be added over time, and any updates will be posted here.
  1. 1

    Registered with the Montana Secretary of State

    Your business must be registered and locatable on the official Montana Secretary of State business search at biz.sosmt.gov/search/business. This keeps the directory legitimate and gives every listed business a verifiable record.

  2. 2

    A working website

    You need a real website we can link to. It doesn't have to be elaborate — a single, well-built page is fine — but a Facebook page, an Instagram profile, or a Linktree alone doesn't count. The whole point here is backlinks and discoverability, and that requires a destination of your own. Suggested: Go to GoDaddy to get a domain. Then, create a website either with GoDaddy, GitHub Pages(Static Only), or Shopify.

  3. 3

    An email tied to that website's domain

    We require a business email that uses your website's domain (for example, hello@yourbusiness.com, not a generic Gmail address). It's a low bar that helps confirm the business and the website actually belong to the same operation. You can have GoDaddy host it when setting up the domain, or you can have Google or iCloud host it.
