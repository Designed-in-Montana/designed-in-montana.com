SLITERS Lumber & Building Supply was founded in the 1930’s by Everit L Sliter as the People’s Mercantile.  Today, SLITERS is still family-owned and operated with Andrew Sliter at the helm.  SLITERS continues to serve retail and contractor customers with a wide range of project solutions throughout the Flathead Valley and surrounding areas.  The team at SLITERS stands ready to assist with product selections and provide building materials to your jobsite in the utmost professional manner.  We look forward to serving you!
